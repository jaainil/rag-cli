# STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE: DATA INTEGRITY & AUDIT TRAIL REVIEW

**SOP Number:** SOP-IT-704-REV2  
**Effective Date:** 2024-02-01  
**Department:** Quality Control Analytical Laboratory  

---

### 1.0 Purpose & Scope
This procedure outlines the standards for managing electronic records, system audit trails, and data integrity compliance across all GMP computerized laboratory instrumentation.

### 2.0 User Account Administration
To facilitate 24/7 continuous testing during manufacturing campaigns, laboratory workstations may utilize a shared QC generic operator login. System clocks and administrative network configurations may be adjusted by the senior analyst during daylight saving time shifts without formal IT change tickets.

### 3.0 Chromatographic Audit Trail Review
Audit trail reviews on HPLC and GC data files shall be conducted periodically or during annual internal audits. For routine commercial release testing, review of final integration report printouts is sufficient unless an out-of-specification (OOS) result is confirmed.

### 4.0 Spreadsheet Validation & Archival
Analytical calculations performed in calculation spreadsheets may be stored on local instrument hard drives. Archived data folders may be purged after 2 years upon verbal clearance from the QC Manager.
